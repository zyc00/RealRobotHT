N·m of joint torque (norm over the three joints) per 1 N push / 1 N·m twist. Pushes are felt through lever arms, so their wrist τ tops out near the wrist-to-hand distance; twists are felt directly (≤ 1).
Three joints resolve only three channels. Components that are each "felt" are not each identifiable: a push along one axis and a twist about another can produce the same wrist torques (the push + twist blind wrenches below say exactly which pairs alias). Under a "hand pushes, doesn't twist" prior the wrist yields 2 push directions; twist z is a check that the prior holds.
