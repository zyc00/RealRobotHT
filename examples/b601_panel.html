B601 reference on Piper

Constant gravity correction. B601 observer, full shaping matrix and 30 ms velocity filter.

One breakaway fraction applies equally to leader J1–J6. A value of 1.0 means each joint's full fraction, still multiplied by friction scale and subject to torque caps. The observer selects direction.

Leader friction scale multiplies kinetic, viscous and breakaway compensation. At zero, breakaway is also off. It does not set follower friction compensation.

Firmware kd is set at launch; its physical units on Piper have not been calibrated.

Follower speeds affect mirroring only, not startup positioning. Increase firmware speed gradually; a faster target ramp can increase tracking error.

MIT follower only: Kp/Kd are raw firmware gains, shared across six joints. Gain changes ramp gradually. No follower gravity feed-forward; sag is possible. Firmware speed % does not apply to MIT.
